## License and source notes

- The Unsplash License permits commercial use and does not require attribution. The license text is linked above for every image.
- Each source URL is the exact Unsplash CDN photo endpoint used to identify and fetch that image. The downloaded derivative URL, crop settings, local path, alt text, and placement are also recorded in [`src/data/imageCatalog.json`](./src/data/imageCatalog.json).
- The individual Unsplash photo pages and photographer names could not be independently retrieved from the CDN identifiers. They are not represented as verified here; Unsplash attribution is not required by the license.
- The site uses locally hosted WebP files at 800 × 533 and 1200 × 800. Source photos are center/focal-cropped to a landscape frame before conversion; page CSS applies the existing responsive `object-fit` treatment.
- These are contextual stock visuals, not screenshots of DocuForge interfaces or claims about tool output.
